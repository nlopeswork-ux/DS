<h2 id="family-and-weights" className="mt-4 mb-6 text-2xl font-semibold">Family & Weights</h2>

A single family, **Inter**, applied in two semantic roles: `font-display` (headings/highlighted numbers) and `font-body` (running text). Four available weights:

<div className="flex flex-col gap-md border border-secondary rounded-lg p-6 mb-8">
  <p className="text-lg font-normal">Regular (400) — Aa Bb Cc</p>
  <p className="text-lg font-medium">Medium (500) — Aa Bb Cc</p>
  <p className="text-lg font-semibold">Semibold (600) — Aa Bb Cc</p>
  <p className="text-lg font-bold">Bold (700) — Aa Bb Cc</p>
</div>

<h2 id="headings" className="mt-16 mb-6 text-2xl font-semibold">Headings (Display)</h2>

Scale from `display-xs` to `display-2xl`, used for section titles and highlighted numbers (e.g. Progress circle). From `display-lg` up, *letter-spacing* tightens to `-2%`.

<Typeset
  fontSizes={[24, 30, 36, 48, 60, 72]}
  fontWeight={600}
  sampleText="Design System"
  fontFamily="var(--font-family-display)"
/>

<h2 id="body" className="mt-16 mb-6 text-2xl font-semibold">Body (Text)</h2>

Scale from `text-xs` to `text-xl`, used for body copy, labels and form component values.

<Typeset
  fontSizes={[12, 14, 16, 18, 20]}
  fontWeight={400}
  sampleText="Supporting text for paragraphs, labels and hints."
  fontFamily="var(--font-family-body)"
/>

<h2 id="caption" className="mt-16 mb-6 text-2xl font-semibold">Caption</h2>

`text-xs` in `semibold` — used for Tooltip titles and other compact labels.

<Typeset
  fontSizes={[12]}
  fontWeight={600}
  sampleText="Caption / Tooltip Title"
  fontFamily="var(--font-family-body)"
/>
