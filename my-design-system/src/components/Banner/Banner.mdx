# Banner

A message that spans the whole page or a section. Communicates information, success, risk or failure that affects the page, without necessarily requiring a workflow interruption. It stays visible until dismissed or resolved. Shares tokens with Alert.

<h2 id="ux-guidelines" className="text-2xl font-semibold">UX Guidelines & When to Use</h2>

Eight variants are defined: Info, Error, Warning, Success × Desktop, Mobile. There is no Size property — the component is sized by content and breakpoint.

### Actions
**Info** uses a secondary `md` Dismiss and a primary `md` action: side by side on desktop, stacked with the primary action first on mobile. **Error, Warning and Success** use two small links: Dismiss and a result-oriented action. Primary and Secondary button styles apply only to Info.

### When to use
- **Info** — news, changes or contextual information without failure. Actions: "View changes", "Learn more".
- **Success** — confirmation of a completed operation that affects the page or section. Actions: view the result.
- **Warning** — risks, limitations or conditions that may cause a problem. Actions: review the configuration, update data.
- **Error** — an operation failed or a problem needs correcting. Actions: "Try again", "View details".
- **Presentation choice** — a Banner for a message that spans the page or a section; an Alert for a delimited area; a Toast for brief feedback that can disappear on its own.

### What to avoid
- Multiple banners competing for attention without clear priority.
- Using Warning for past failures.
- Using a banner for feedback about a single field or card.
- Treating "Dismiss" as "Resolve" or "Undo".

<h2 id="anatomy-best-practices" className="text-2xl font-semibold">Anatomy & Best Practices</h2>

### Structure
- **Featured icon** — a 32px tinted circle with a semantic glyph, also on mobile.
- **Title and supporting text** — inline on desktop (wrapping when needed), stacked on mobile. Supporting text may contain an inline link.
- **Actions** — see above. On mobile they move below the text.
- **Close (X) button** — optional; pinned top-right on mobile.

### Recommended behavior
Recommendations, not verified runtime behavior: show one global banner at a time, prioritized Error, Warning, Success, Info. Critical banners may hide both X and Dismiss (`showCloseButton={false}` and `dismissLabel=""`). Never auto-dismiss and never trap focus.
1. Place the banner at the top of the affected page or section without blocking the page or applying a focus trap.
2. Do not auto-dismiss banners. Critical messages may hide X and Dismiss so they remain visible until resolved.
3. The close button ("X") and "Dismiss" dismiss the message without undoing operations or solving the underlying problem.
4. Use labels that describe the result (e.g., "Try again", "View changes") and prevent duplicate submissions during asynchronous tasks.
5. Do not rely solely on color; combine text and semantic icons, and ensure accessible names for close buttons and visible focus for actions.

<h2 id="accessibility" className="text-2xl font-semibold">Accessibility (a11y)</h2>
- **Semantics:** for dynamic content, `role="alert"` for Error and `role="status"` for the other colors (the defaults).
- **Present on page load:** pass `role="region"` and an accessible `aria-label` instead.
- **Not only color:** every banner pairs its color with a semantic icon and text.
- **Close button:** icon-only, with an `aria-label` (default "Dismiss banner").
- **Focus:** actions and the close button have a visible focus ring; the banner is never a focus trap.
