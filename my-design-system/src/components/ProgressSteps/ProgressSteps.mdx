export const ExampleGallery = ({ tabs }) => {

  return (
    <div className="my-6 rounded-lg border border-secondary overflow-hidden">
      <div role="tablist" className="flex gap-1 border-b border-secondary bg-secondary px-2 pt-2">
        {tabs.map((tab, i) => (
          <button
            key={tab.label}
            type="button"
            role="tab"
            aria-selected={i === active}
            onClick={() => setActive(i)}
            className={cn(
              'rounded-t-md border border-b-0 border-transparent px-4 py-2 -mb-px text-sm font-semibold',
              i === active ? 'bg-primary text-primary border-secondary' : 'text-tertiary hover:text-secondary',
            )}
          >
            {tab.label}
          </button>
        ))}
      </div>
      <div className="relative isolate bg-primary p-6">{tabs[active].content}</div>
    </div>
  );
};

# Progress Steps

Shows where someone is in a multi-step flow such as onboarding, checkout or a long form. Each step is complete, current or still to do, with an optional title and supporting text. Run it horizontally or vertically, with text or as icons only, in three sizes: it covers wide pages, narrow panels and compact headers.

<h2 id="ux-guidelines" className="text-2xl font-semibold">UX Guidelines & When to Use</h2>

### When to use
- A flow of **a known, short number of steps** (roughly 3 to 6) that people complete in order.
- When knowing how far along they are, and what is left, reduces drop-off.

### When not to use
- Navigation between independent sections: use Tabs.
- Page-by-page browsing of a list: use Pagination.
- A single measurable quantity such as an upload or a quota: use a progress bar.

### Choosing a layout
Pick the two decisions separately:

- **Orientation.** Horizontal for full-width pages and headers. Vertical for side panels and narrow columns. **Auto** (the default) is horizontal when the component's own container is 512px or wider and vertical below, so the same markup works on a desktop page, in a side panel and on a phone.
- **Content.** *Text* shows a title and supporting text per step, with the icon above (horizontal) or beside (vertical) it. *Minimal* shows the icons alone, for compact headers; turn on **Step label** to add "Step n of N" when the step names are shown elsewhere.
- **Connector.** The line joining the icons, on by default. Turn it off for a lighter look.

<h2 id="anatomy-best-practices" className="text-2xl font-semibold">Anatomy & Best Practices</h2>

### Structure
- **Step icon:** a 24, 32 or 40px circle. Incomplete is outlined with a grey dot, current is solid brand with a white dot and a ring, complete is solid brand with a check (the `check` glyph from the Icon set).
- **Title and supporting text:** semibold title and regular supporting text. The current step uses the brand text colors, the others `text-secondary` and `text-tertiary`.
- **Connector:** a 2px line, horizontal between icons or vertical below them. It is brand colored between two steps that are already done and `bg-quaternary` after the current step.
- **"Step n of N" label:** `text-sm` medium, only with `content="minimal"`.

### Interactions
- Status comes from `currentStep`: earlier steps are complete, later ones incomplete. The component never decides when a step is valid; the flow does.
- Pass `onStepClick` to make each step a button. Decide in the flow which steps may be reopened (usually only completed ones).

### Best practices
- Keep step titles to one or two words and supporting text to one short line.
- Do not mix layouts or sizes within the same flow.
- Repeat the current step name as the page heading below the indicator.

<h2 id="accessibility" className="text-2xl font-semibold">Accessibility (a11y)</h2>
- **Landmark:** a `nav` labelled "Progress" (override with `aria-label`) around an ordered list.
- **Current step:** `aria-current="step"` on the current item. Status is never color alone: every step also has a hidden "(completed)", "(current step)" or "(not started)" text.
- **Minimal content:** the step title is exposed to screen readers even though it is not shown.
- **Keyboard:** with `onStepClick` every step is a native button (Tab, Enter, Space) with a visible focus ring.
- **Contrast:** the brand-700 title on the page background and white on brand-600 both pass WCAG AA.
