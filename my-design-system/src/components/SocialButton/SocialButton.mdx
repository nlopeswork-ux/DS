<h2 id="ux-guidelines" className="text-2xl font-semibold">UX Guidelines & When to Use</h2>

### ✅ When to use
- On sign-in/sign-up screens, as an alternative to email/password authentication.
- Grouped together (see `SocialButtonGroup`) when several providers are offered — always stacked vertically, never side by side in a row of full-width buttons.
- Icon-only (`supportingText: false`) in compact spaces where the provider set is already familiar to the user (e.g. a toolbar or a secondary settings panel).

### 🚫 When not to use
- As a generic action button — a `Button` communicates decision hierarchy through fill/hierarchy; `SocialButton` always maps 1:1 to a specific identity provider.
- With a provider not on the supported list (`Google`, `Facebook`, `Apple`, `Twitter`, `Figma`, `Dribbble`) — extend the component's `social` union instead of hardcoding a one-off variant.

<h2 id="anatomy-best-practices" className="text-2xl font-semibold">Anatomy & Best Practices</h2>

### Label (`supportingText`)
- ✅ The label is generated automatically from the provider — "Sign in with Google", "Sign in with X" — never pass free text; the copy must stay consistent across every provider button in the product.
- ✅ Use `supportingText: false` only when space is constrained and the provider is identifiable by its logo alone (e.g. a compact row of icon buttons).
- 🚫 Don't mix labeled and icon-only buttons within the same `SocialButtonGroup` — pick one style per group (see `style: 'Buttons' | 'Icons'`).

### Icon
- The provider logo is always decorative (`aria-hidden`) — when `supportingText` is off, the button itself carries an `aria-label` ("Sign in with …") so the accessible name isn't lost.
- Figma and Google keep their full-color mark even on the `Brand` theme — Figma's official logo is inherently multicolor, and Google's Brand button uses a white/outline fill rather than a solid brand color.

See the provider, theme and icon-only examples in the tab gallery under [UX Guidelines](#ux-guidelines).

<h2 id="accessibility" className="text-2xl font-semibold">Accessibility (a11y)</h2>
- **Contrast:** every theme/provider pair meets WCAG AA (ratio ≥ 4.5:1) for both text and icon against their background.
- **Accessible name:** with `supportingText: true`, the visible text is the accessible name. With `supportingText: false`, the button carries an explicit `aria-label` ("Sign in with …") so screen readers still announce the provider.
- **Visible focus:** the focus ring (2px white halo + 4px color ring) must never be disabled — it's the primary visual cue for keyboard navigation.
- **Keyboard navigation:** SocialButton renders a native `<button>` — reachable with `Tab`, activatable with `Enter`/`Space`, with no need for extra `tabIndex` or `role`.
