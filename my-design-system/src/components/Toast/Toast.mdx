# Toast

Brief, temporary messages that confirm the result of an action without interrupting the user's flow. They appear over the interface, stay visible for a few seconds and dismiss on their own — except errors, which stay until dismissed. For failures that persist or messages that need attention, use an Alert or a Banner instead.

<h2 id="ux-guidelines" className="text-2xl font-semibold">UX Guidelines & When to Use</h2>

Toasts confirm the result of an action or share light, passive information without interrupting the workflow. They stay brief and scannable: a semantic icon, a short title, optional supporting text, and **at most one action**. They are visually elevated above the interface. The Playground preview is persistent so it doesn't vanish while you experiment — try the Interactive tab for the real timer.

### When to use
- **Info** — light, passive updates that need no decision, such as a finished background task or a copied link. Example action: "View".
- **Success** — confirmation of a completed operation, such as saved changes or a sent message. Actions: "Undo", "View profile".
- **Warning** — light, non-urgent risks that do not require immediate action, such as an unstable connection. Dismisses after about 8 seconds.
- **Error** — only transient, recoverable failures that do not block the user, such as a failed save that can be retried. Does not dismiss automatically and should offer "Try again". If the problem persists or the user must act on it, use an Alert or a Banner.
- **Presentation choice** — a Toast for brief feedback that can disappear without consequence; an Alert for a delimited area; a Banner for a page or section.

### What to avoid
- Using a toast for failures that persist or that block the user.
- Putting an essential action inside a toast.
- Showing several toasts at once without a limit.
- Confirming success before the operation is really complete.
- Treating the "X" as "Resolve" or "Undo".

<h2 id="anatomy-best-practices" className="text-2xl font-semibold">Anatomy & Best Practices</h2>

### Structure
- **Featured icon** — a bare 20px glyph with two soft rings (30% / 10%) in the toast colour.
- **Title** — short; optional supporting text below.
- **Action** — at most one small link. On mobile it sits below the content.
- **Close (X) button** — optional. On mobile the icon and the X share a top row.
- **Elevation** — `shadow-lg`; 384px wide on desktop (min 320px), full width on mobile.

### Recommended behavior
These recommendations describe the intended runtime behavior (implemented by `Toast` and `ToastProvider`).

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr><th>Behavior</th><th>Recommendation</th></tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td><strong>Auto-dismiss</strong></td><td>5s without action, 8s with action, never under 4s. Warning toasts dismiss after about 8 seconds. Error stays until dismissed.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><strong>Pause</strong></td><td>Pause the timer while the toast is hovered or contains focus, and resume when the user leaves.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><strong>Max visible</strong></td><td>Show a maximum of three toasts at a time, with the most recent closest to the screen edge. Do not stack duplicates; restart the timer of the existing toast instead.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><strong>Position</strong></td><td>Desktop upper-right, mobile screen bottom.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><strong>ARIA</strong></td><td><code>role="status"</code> polite for Info, Success, and Warning; <code>role="alert"</code> assertive for Error.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><strong>Focus</strong></td><td>Never move focus to the toast. Ensure visible action focus and accessibly named close.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><strong>Reduced motion</strong></td><td>Respect reduced motion preferences by using a simple fade instead of a slide.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><strong>Action availability</strong></td><td>The action is also available elsewhere because temporary toasts disappear.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><strong>Essential actions</strong></td><td>Avoid essential or unique actions in a temporary message and prevent duplicate async submissions.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><strong>Dismiss X</strong></td><td>Dismiss X does not undo or resolve the underlying problem.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<h2 id="accessibility" className="text-2xl font-semibold">Accessibility (a11y)</h2>
- **Live region:** `role="status"` (polite) for Info, Success and Warning; `role="alert"` (assertive) for Error.
- **Focus:** never moved to the toast; the action and close button have a visible focus ring.
- **Not only color:** every toast pairs its color with a semantic icon and text.
- **Close button:** icon-only, with an `aria-label` (default "Dismiss notification").
- **Motion:** a slide in, or a simple fade under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Timer:** pauses while hovered or focused, so keyboard and pointer users are never rushed.

> Implementation note: the featured icon and close (X) button are shared internal placeholders (`FeedbackShared`) until their own components exist.
